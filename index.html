    How a lean becomes a best bet

    • The model and at least two independent signals agree
    • The line hasn't moved against us
    • No correlated best bet in the same game
    • Confidence tiers: Solid is a good bet with a real edge, Strong adds strong supporting signals, and Top play is rare. Every tier is a bet worth making; leans sit below Solid. Confidence is separate from edge size.

    What we learned

    • 8–1 with best bets is a great week, but on average we got the same numbers the market closed at, so most of that was the ball bouncing our way, not proof of an edge.
    • Best call: 49ers −3, which closed at −4 and won by 25. Worst call: Jets/Chargers under, where the total rose 2 points before kickoff and the game went over.
    • The Monday night under (a lean) won easily; the one tracked prop, Okonkwo under 43.5 yards, lost.
    • We tested whether certain college totals edge sizes win more often. They don't reliably: the 'best' size changed from one season to the next, so we're not sizing up on any of them.

    How the model works

    Three models project every game from opponent-adjusted efficiency, meaning how well each offense and defense has played given who it played. A projection only becomes a pick when a second, independent reason agrees with it.
